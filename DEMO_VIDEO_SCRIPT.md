# MEGA.TALK Demo Video Script

## Runtime
~90 seconds

## 1. Hook
"Most sales teams can tell you how many calls happened. Almost none can tell you which conversations actually create pipeline."

## 2. Product framing
"MEGA.TALK is the data-based sales enabler for modern outbound teams. It turns raw call activity into observable coaching signals, guide adherence, and outcome patterns leadership can actually use."

## 3. Dashboard scene
"Here on the dashboard, we start with anonymized ground-truth call data. Not vanity metrics — real signal mix, callback intent, talk-time patterns, and outcome distribution. You can instantly see where momentum is building and where the motion is leaking."

## 4. Training scene
"Next, we switch into the live training arena. A rep speaks to an AI persona that behaves like a real revenue-operations buyer. While the conversation happens, MEGA.TALK measures the guide in real time — not just whether a call happened, but whether the right moments happened."

## 5. Analysis scene
"After the call, the analysis layer shows exactly what moved the conversation forward: which steps landed, which ones were missed, and what evidence in the transcript supports the score. This is where coaching stops being subjective."

## 6. Data story
"And because the app is grounded in anonymized historical call data, leadership can connect training behavior to actual sales outcomes — callback intent, positive conversations, tag hygiene, and review cadence."

## 7. Close
"That’s the point of MEGA.TALK: not another dashboard, not another call recorder — a system that makes sales conversations measurable, coachable, and operationally useful."
